Online Scams

Passwords & 2FAStrong PasswordsTwo-Factor AuthenticationPhishing & ScamsSuspicious EmailsOnline Scams

Online scams often create pressure by promising a prize, threatening a problem, offering a deal that seems too good to be true, or asking for immediate payment. Before sending money or information, verify who you are dealing with and avoid acting only because a message says it is urgent.

Protect yourself from pressure

A legitimate organization should give you time to verify a request. Be especially careful when someone demands unusual payment methods.

Key points

  • Verify the person or organization independently.
  • Be skeptical of unexpected prizes and giveaways.
  • Do not send money because of threats or pressure.
  • Report suspicious activity through the appropriate official service.
