Passwords & 2FA

Passwords & 2FAStrong PasswordsTwo-Factor AuthenticationPhishing & ScamsSuspicious EmailsOnline Scams

Your password is one of the first lines of defense for an online account. Using a strong and unique password makes it harder for someone to guess or reuse your login information. Two-factor authentication adds another layer of protection by requiring a second step when you sign in.

Two layers are better than one

A strong password protects the login itself, while 2FA can help protect the account even if that password is exposed.

Key points

  • Use a different password for each important account.
  • Choose long passwords or passphrases.
  • Use an authenticator app or security key when possible.
  • Never share verification codes with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.
